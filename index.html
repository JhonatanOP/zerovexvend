From first call to full machine in three steps

Simple written terms and an easy exit if it is not a fit.

Quick visit

We spend 10 minutes at your site to check the space, power outlet and foot traffic, and pick a safe, visible spot.

Pick the menu

We send a two-minute survey by QR code or paper. Your people vote on snacks, drinks and dietary needs.

We install and service

We deliver, level, clean and stock the machine, then check in on day 2, day 7 and day 30.

Why hosts choose a local operator

You talk to the owner, not a call center.

Always stocked

We restock on a set schedule, before the machine runs low, and check expiration dates on every visit.

Problems solved fast

Every machine has our direct number and a QR code for feedback and refunds. If it takes money and fails, the refund is automatic.

Options for every diet

Gluten-free, nut-free, vegan and low-sugar choices, plus halal and kosher-friendly items where we can source them.

Your requests get stocked

A request card on every machine. We review weekly and add the most popular items.

Flexible terms

Swap products, adjust prices or remove the machine with short notice. Agreements are written and simple.

Clean and well kept

Every machine is cleaned, well lit and clearly priced, with our contact sticker on the front so help is one text away.

Common questions

Short answers before you ask.

What does it really cost us?

Nothing. We provide the machine, installation, stocking and maintenance. You provide a spot and access to power.

What if our team does not use it?

That is what the survey is for. We stock what your people asked for, then adjust the menu based on sales and requests. If it is still not working, we remove the machine with short notice.

Who handles broken machines and refunds?

We do. Call or text the number on the machine. We respond within 24 hours, and refunds are processed within one business day.

Can we keep our current vending company?

Yes. Many hosts add one of our machines for healthy options, cold drinks or coffee alongside their current setup.

Do you work with schools and sites with rules?

Yes. We follow your nutrition and vendor guidelines and can support student programs. We also carry the insurance certificate hosts or corporate offices ask for.

Ready for a machine at your location?

Tell us about your site and we will reply within one business day.
